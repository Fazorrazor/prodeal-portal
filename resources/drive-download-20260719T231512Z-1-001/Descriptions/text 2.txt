Design products and descriptions 

1. Sand Fixing Agent
	•	Description: An acrylic-based, deep-penetrating liquid primer designed to bind loose, powdery particles on weak walls and floors. It goes on as a milky white fluid but dries completely clear. 
	•	Specifications:
	•	Composition: Water-based acrylic polymer.
	•	Coverage: Approximately 6–8 m² per kilogram (depending on surface porosity).
	•	Drying Time: Touch dry in 1–2 hours; fully cured in 12–24 hours. 
	•	What It Does: It penetrates deeply into crumbling concrete, chalky plaster, or dusty screed, gluing the loose sand particles together from the inside out. This hardens the surface, stops chalking, and creates a solid, stable base so that paint, putty, or waterproof coatings can adhere properly without peeling off.

2. Liquid Rubber Waterproof Coating
	•	Description: A highly flexible, heavy-duty elastomeric sealant that cures into a seamless, rubberized membrane to block water and air infiltration. 
	•	Specifications:
	•	Composition: Modified asphalt-emulsion or water-based polyurethane.
	•	Elongation: 900% to 1200% (highly stretchy).
	•	Curing Time: 2–4 hours between coats; 24–48 hours for a full cure. 
	•	What It Does: It seals roofs, foundations, gutters, and retaining walls against severe leaks. Because of its extreme elasticity, it bridges hairline cracks and expands or contracts with hot and cold weather shifts without cracking, peeling, or degrading under UV sunlight. 

3. Transparent Waterproof Coating
	•	Description: A clear, invisible waterproof film designed to seal surfaces without altering their original look, color, or texture. 
	•	Specifications:
	•	Composition: Silicone, silane-siloxane, or clear polyurethane.
	•	Finish: Completely transparent (invisible matte or slight gloss finish).
	•	Drying Time: 1–3 hours per coat; waterproof functionality activates within 24 hours. 
	•	What It Does: It stops water penetration on visible finished surfaces like exterior brick walls, tiled bathroom floors, balconies, and exposed concrete. It creates a hydrophobic (water-repelling) barrier that forces water to bead up and roll away, preventing internal dampness, mold growth, and tile-joint leaks while keeping the original design fully visible. 

4. Epoxy Floor Paint
	•	Description: A tough, two-part resin coating system (resin + hardener) engineered to protect concrete floors and give them a seamless, high-gloss, industrial-strength finish. 
	•	Specifications:
	•	Composition: Two-component epoxy resin.
	•	Durability: Highly resistant to heavy impact, chemicals, oils, and vehicle traffic.
	•	Curing Time: Pot life (working time once mixed) is 30–45 minutes; foot traffic allowed in 24 hours; full chemical cure takes 7 days. 
	•	What It Does: It transforms weak, dusty concrete floors in garages, warehouses, workshops, or commercial spaces into a mirror-like, ultra-durable surface. It resists car tire marks, chemical spills, and heavy foot traffic, making the floor completely dust-proof and very easy to wipe clean. 

5. Self-Leveling Sealant
	•	Description: A fluid, pourable joint sealant that automatically flattens out under its own weight when poured into horizontal gaps, eliminating the need for manual tooling or smoothing. 
	•	Specifications:
	•	Composition: Polyurethane, silicone, or MS polymer base.
	•	Joint Movement Capability: Accommodates ±25% to ±50% joint expansion and contraction.
	•	Drying Time: Skin formation in 1–2 hours; cures downward at a rate of roughly 2–3mm every 24 hours. 
	•	What It Does: It fills and seals horizontal expansion joints, cracks, and gaps in concrete driveways, sidewalks, balconies, and warehouse floors. Because it flows naturally into every corner of the crack before curing into a tough, rubbery seal, it effectively blocks water from seeping beneath the concrete slabs, preventing erosion and structural sinking.

6. Dessini K11 Universal Waterproof Coating
	•	Description: A two-part, polymer-modified cementitious waterproofing slurry designed to provide a highly rigid, water-blocking layer for concrete structures. 
	•	Specifications:
	•	Composition: Two-component kit containing a synthetic resin liquid polymer and a cement-based powder mix.
	•	Color: Typically distinct Blue (helps track application thickness visually).
	•	Coverage: Roughly 3 m² per 5 kg kit.
	•	Drying Time: 2–4 hours between coats; full curing within 3–7 days. 
	•	What It Does: It creates an indestructible barrier against both positive and negative water pressure. It is specifically formulated to seal internal wet areas like bathrooms, toilets, kitchens, and water tanksbefore tiling. It bonds aggressively to damp concrete and will not peel off under stagnant water. 

7. Dessini Thermal Insulation & Sunscreen Roof Coating
	•	Description: An acrylic-based liquid roof membrane designed to offer dual protection—waterproofing against rain and heat insulation against the sun. 
	•	Specifications:
	•	Composition: Solar-reflective acrylic emulsion with micro-hollow ceramic beads.
	•	Color: Highly reflective White.
	•	Curing Time: Touch dry in 2 hours; fully cures in 24 hours. 
	•	What It Does: It protects exposed flat concrete roofs and metal roofing sheets from leaks. Simultaneously, it reflects up to 85% of solar radiation. This lowers the roof’s surface temperature significantly, which reduces indoor building heat and lowers air conditioning costs. 

8. Dessini Anti-Rust Conversion Agent
	•	Description: A chemically reactive primer fluid designed to treat rusted iron and steel building components, neutralizing rust without requiring extensive sandblasting. 
	•	Specifications:
	•	Composition: Water-based organic acid polymer.
	•	Appearance: Milky liquid that cures into a black, stable protective layer.
	•	Drying Time: 20–30 minutes to touch; cures in 24 hours. 
	•	What It Does: When brushed directly over rusted metal, it converts iron oxide (rust) into a stable, non-corrosive chemical compound. It acts as a hard primer that permanently stops the rust from spreading, making the metal surface ready to be painted with standard industrial topcoats. 

9. Dessini Tile Adhesive Liquid Additive
	•	Description: A high-performance liquid latex modifier engineered to mix with standard cement or tile grouts to dramatically improve their stickiness and water resistance. 
	•	Specifications:
	•	Composition: Concentrated Styrene-Butadiene Rubber (SBR) latex.
	•	Pot Life: Extends grout working time by 1–2 hours. 
	•	What It Does: It transforms basic cement into a flexible, heavy-duty tile glue. It prevents tiles from cracking or popping loose in high-moisture zones like swimming pools, exterior building facades, and showers.
