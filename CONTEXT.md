# CONTEXT

**Purpose:** Maintains the high-level business context, target audience, brand identity, and primary objectives of the Pro Deal Industries portal.

---

## 1. Business Overview

**Brand:** Pro Deal Industries
**Identity:** A large-scale B2B industrial supplier operating a multi-division inquiry portal.
**Objective:** Provide a guest-only B2B inquiry portal where clients from 4 distinct industrial divisions submit quote requests that are instantly routed to the correct staff member via WhatsApp, with no client login required at any point.

---

## 2. Platform Objectives

The platform serves strictly as a B2B functional business tool, never as a consumer e-commerce cart.

1. **Facilitate Inquiries:** Clients browse catalogs and submit structured quote requests for specific products or custom fabrication.
2. **Streamline Operations:** Quote requests are instantly delivered to division-specific staff via the Meta WhatsApp Business API.
3. **Tracking & Transparency:** Guests receive a `tracking_uuid` to track their inquiry status without ever creating an account.

---

## 3. The 4 Divisions

The portal serves 4 distinct divisions. They are NOT interchangeable.

### 1. 3D SIGNAGES (Project-Based)
- **Audience:** Business owners, marketing managers, contractors.
- **Rules:** Requires file upload for artwork. Focus on custom dimensions, materials, and deadlines.

### 2. SOUVENIRS & PRINTING (Order-Based)
- **Audience:** Event planners, HR departments, schools.
- **Rules:** Requires product type, quantity, and print specs. Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) must be visible.

### 3. DISPOSABLE BOWLS (Inventory-Based)
- **Audience:** Caterers, restaurants, retailers.
- **Rules:** Live inventory tracking. Out of stock disables quote CTA. Delivery address required. 

### 4. CHEMICALS (Technical/Compliance-Based)
- **Audience:** Manufacturers, laboratories, processors.
- **Rules:** "Intended Use" is legally required. Safety Data Sheets (SDS) via signed URLs. CAS numbers and Grade selectors required.

---

## 4. Brand Tone & Voice

- **Tone:** Premium B2B Professionalism. Curated, clean, effortlessly elegant, and highly precise.
- **Avoid:** Consumer SaaS marketing, "Add to cart" language, checkout patterns, or cluttered/dense layouts.
- **Language:** Inquiry, quote request, submission, tracking ID.

---

## 5. Non-Negotiables

- The public user is ALWAYS anonymous. No client authentication.
- The Quote Builder is NOT a shopping cart. Never use payment processing.
- Division awareness is critical. Never treat Chemicals like Souvenirs.
